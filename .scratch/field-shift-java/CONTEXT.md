# Field Shift Java

Terms for the service's area and crop evidence.

## Language

**Kecamatan screening summary**:
A dated area estimate for one administrative *kecamatan*, with stated source coverage. It is not a measurement of each field inside it.
_Avoid_: field result, checked zone result

**Farming zone**:
A named planning area with a checked, dated boundary and a stable ID. It groups local plots and may differ from an administrative *kecamatan*.
_Avoid_: field, NASA cell

**Checked local plot**:
An operational crop unit with a local ID and a boundary checked against local records. It does not establish ownership.
_Avoid_: legal parcel

**FTW predicted field**:
An outline inferred from remote sensing that can guide review. It is not a checked plot or legal parcel.
_Avoid_: verified field, legal parcel

**Exploratory scenario**:
An area-level comparison of example crop plans under stated assumptions, without checked plot evidence. It does not give plot advice.
_Avoid_: checked pilot, recommendation

**Agronomist review aid**:
A comparison of two plans for a checked local plot, with its evidence and missing facts recorded for local review. It can be marked unvalidated until an independent check is complete.
_Avoid_: farmer-facing recommendation

**Farmer–plot link**:
A relationship between a farmer and a checked local plot that becomes active after the farmer agrees and a partner checks it. It gives access to this service; it does not establish land ownership.
_Avoid_: ownership record, selected FTW field

**Farmer plan**:
A crop, planting-window, and water plan for a checked local plot, based on approved local rules and checked data. It becomes farmer-facing advice only after a named local agronomist approves it.
_Avoid_: exploratory scenario, unreviewed recommendation
