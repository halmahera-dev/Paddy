# Set farmer and plot-data access rules

Type: `grilling`  
Status: resolved  
Assignee: heritsam  
Parent: [Field Shift Java decision map](../map.md)
Blocked by: 17

## Question

How will a pilot partner confirm a farmer–plot link and control access to checked plot records, chat questions, review status, and approved plans? Decide who may create, check, correct, and revoke a link; what the farmer and agronomist may see; what consent or partner authority is needed; and how corrections or revoked access affect saved advice. Keep the link separate from land ownership and FTW predicted boundaries.

## Answer

A farmer may request and confirm a link to a checked local plot. The pilot partner must have authority to supply the plot record, and the farmer must agree to its use in this service. A named partner worker checks the local plot record and the farmer's link to it before access becomes active. A farmer's confirmation alone does not activate the link. The link gives access to this service; it is not proof of land ownership, and an FTW predicted outline cannot establish it.

More than one farmer may have an active link to the same plot. The partner checks each link, and each farmer gives their own agreement. Each farmer has a separate chat and plan. A farmer may see their own checked plot facts with source and date, the status of their plan review, and plans approved for them. They may report an error. They may not see draft plans, staff notes, another farmer's chat, or another farmer's plan for the same plot.

Only assigned people may use the records needed for their work. Named partner staff may see what they need to check links and correct records. The assigned agronomist may see the checked facts, chat questions, and plan material needed to review that farmer's plan. Other staff do not get general access to individual plot or chat records.

A farmer may ask for a correction or withdraw agreement at any time. Named partner staff check and make link or plot-record corrections, and may end a link when its evidence fails. An agronomist may flag a problem for partner staff. Withdrawal ends current farmer access at once. A corrected plot fact or changed link stops affected current advice. Keep a dated audit record of the former link and plan, but do not present the old plan as current advice. Any new advice needs a checked active link, a new calculation where affected, and a named agronomist's review under the [farmer plan release gate](./19-set-farmer-plan-release-gate.md).

The user confirmed this full access rule in the live discussion.
